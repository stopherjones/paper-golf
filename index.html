HOW TO PLAY (TRADITIONAL):

  • Driver (1D6+4): Longest drive; scatter roll applied. Tee only.
  • Long Iron (1D6+2): Strong approach; minor scatter. Tee or fairway.
  • Short Iron (1D6): Precise pitch; no scatter. Anywhere on land.
  • Putter (1D6: 1-3): Controlled roll for greens & close approaches.
  • Sand Bunkers: -1 penalty to your distance D6 roll (min 0).
  • Water & Trees (OB): Nearest land drop + 1 penalty stroke.
  • Gimme (+1): Ball adjacent to hole (1 hex) can be finished instantly.
  • Slope Arrows (↑): Slide the ball 1 hex in arrow direction upon landing.

CRAZY GOLF (MINI-GOLF) MODE:

  • Dice Arsenal: Select from Precision (d4), Standard (d6), Super-Bounce (d10), Sticky Lead (ignores slopes), Rubber Bumper (bank ricochets), or Chaos (d20).
  • Spinning Windmill: Alternates OPEN & BLOCKED each stroke! Time your roll to slip past the blades.
  • Warp Tubes: Roll into the cyan portal (IN) to warp instantly out of the exit (OUT) with forward momentum.
  • Speed Ramps: Catapults the ball +2 distance across water chasms onto the upper deck.
  • Bumper Rails: Striking perimeter walls rebounds the ball safely instead of going out of bounds.
  • Loop-de-Loop: Roll into the spiral funnel with speed to loop directly toward the cup.
